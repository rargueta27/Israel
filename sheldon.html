The Legacy of Sheldon Adelson

Honoring the vision of a man who built empires and strengthened the heart of the Jewish people.

From Entrepreneur to Visionary

Sheldon Adelson's story began in a modest neighborhood in Boston, but his ambition knew no borders. By founding the Las Vegas Sands Corp, he revolutionized the concept of the integrated resort. However, his business success was always a means to a greater end: the preservation and celebration of Jewish heritage.

A Philanthropic Giant

Alongside his wife Miriam, Sheldon became the driving force behind Birthright Israel, providing a bridge for young Jews across the globe to return to their roots. His contributions to Yad Vashem and countless Israeli security and medical initiatives ensure that his name will forever be linked to the "Eternal Return."
