Dr. Miriam Adelson

Physician, Philanthropist, and Guardian of the Jewish Future

A Life of Healing and Purpose

Born in Tel Aviv in 1945, Miriam Farbstein grew up in the fledgling State of Israel, a background that deeply rooted her commitment to the Jewish people. After earning her medical degree from Tel Aviv University's Sackler Medical School, she specialized in internal and emergency medicine. Her career has been defined by a unique intersection of scientific rigor and deep humanitarian empathy.

The Fight Against Addiction

One of Dr. Adelson's most significant contributions to global health is her pioneering work in the field of addiction. She founded the Dr. Miriam and Sheldon G. Adelson Research Clinic in Tel Aviv and its counterpart in Las Vegas. Her clinics have helped thousands overcome chemical dependency through research-based treatments, treating addiction as a disease rather than a moral failing.

A Global Philanthropist

Alongside her late husband, Sheldon Adelson, Dr. Miriam Adelson has become one of the most impactful philanthropists in history. Her legacy is etched into the landscape of modern Israel and the United States:

  • Birthright Israel (Taglit): Strengthening Jewish identity by bringing hundreds of thousands of young adults to the Holy Land.
  • Medical Advancement: Funding the Adelson School of Medicine and massive research centers to combat life-threatening diseases.
  • Educational Leadership: Supporting the Dr. Miriam and Sheldon G. Adelson Educational Campus, ensuring the next generation is equipped with knowledge and Jewish values.

The Presidential Medal of Freedom

In 2018, Dr. Adelson was awarded the Presidential Medal of Freedom, the highest civilian honor in the United States. This recognized her profound dedication to medical research and her tireless advocacy for the US-Israel relationship, cementing her place as a global leader in the Jewish world.
