Israel Forver: The Eternal Return

Honoring the legacy of Sheldon and Miriam Adelson through the rhythm of our history.

The Roman Displacement

Following the destruction of the Second Temple by the Roman Empire, the Jewish people were forcibly displaced from their ancestral land. For two millennia, the Diaspora spread across every corner of the globe, yet the heart of the nation remained in Jerusalem.

The Shoah (The Holocaust)

In the darkest chapter of human history, the Jewish people faced an existential threat. Six million souls were lost, but the spirit of survival and the yearning for Zion could not be extinguished.

The Eternal Return

Through unwavering hard work, resilience, and faith, the Jewish people returned to their home. We have bloomed the desert, built world-leading technology, and secured a future for generations to come.
